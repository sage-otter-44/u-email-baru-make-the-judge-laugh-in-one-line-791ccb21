One funny line, written to `/output/entry.txt` by `main.ts` (Node 22, no dependencies, no network).

> My dog learned to open the fridge, so I installed a child lock, and now he just stares at me like I'm the one who can't be trusted with food.

141 characters. A concrete scene with the laugh landing on the last clause: the roles reverse and the dog ends up judging the human.

Run: `node main.ts` — writes `/output/entry.txt`, with a built-in 1–200 character gate check.
